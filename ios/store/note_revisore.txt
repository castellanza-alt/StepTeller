NEAT reads today's step count from Apple Health. Access is read-only: the app never writes to Health, makes no network requests and has no account, so no login or demo account is needed.

If the test device has no step data in Health (or Health access is not granted), the Home screen shows 0 steps with a notice. Steps can then be entered manually by tapping the step number.

The app is free, with no in-app purchases, no ads and no tracking.
