Thank you for reviewing NEAT - Every Step Counts. NEAT has no account, login, user-generated content or paid content.

1. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
NEAT is a simple daily step-goal app for anyone who wants to walk more, outdoors or on a treadmill: today's steps, the steps left to the goal, and the walking minutes needed at the chosen speed. Each day at goal extends a streak; every 15 days in a row the user earns a "Joker" that saves the streak if a day is missed. History shows totals, averages and records. NEAT makes no medical or health claims.

2. HOW TO USE THE APP
- No login, account or demo credentials are needed.
- First launch: a 5-page introduction. Page 2 asks for read-only Apple Health access. Page 3 sets the daily goal. Page 4 chooses NEAT's voice (see NEW IN 1.3). Page 5 turns the evening reminder on or off; notification permission is requested when the user taps START.
- Home: today's steps, steps left and walking minutes. Drag along the dashed road line to change the speed. With no Health data, Home shows "NO DATA FROM HEALTH" and an OPEN SETTINGS button.
- Top bar: the red button on the left opens History (and goes back to Home); the button on the right opens Settings.
- Settings: goal, Voice, Notifications, Streak & Jokers, units, theme, Backup (JSON file via share sheet and file picker), Privacy, Support, Replay introduction.
- Widgets: Home Screen (small, medium) and Lock Screen.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple HealthKit, read-only: steps, walking + running distance, walking/running workouts (to fit the minutes to the user's pace). NEAT never writes to Health.
- Local notifications only (no push server): evening reminder, goal and Joker alerts, NEAT's lines.
- WidgetKit, with an App Group shared by the app and its widgets.
- iCloud key-value storage in the user's own account: settings, goal history and streak start. No Health data in iCloud; the developer has no access.
- A static public text file of NEAT's lines, downloaded once a day from our GitHub Pages site (https://castellanza-alt.github.io/StepTeller/neat/nasty/<language>/battute.json), only when the voice is Spicy or No mercy. A plain GET with no parameters, cookies or identifiers: no user data is sent. Without it, the lines inside the app are used.
No servers that receive data, analytics, ads, AI services, sign-in or payment providers. No data is sent to the developer.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Same everywhere, except language (10 localizations, English fallback) and default unit (miles in US/UK, km elsewhere).

5. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. Not a medical device; no third-party protected material; fonts under the SIL Open Font License.

NEW IN 1.3
- NEAT has a voice, with three levels: Normal (silent, the app as before), Spicy (sarcasm, no swearing) and No mercy. The user chooses in the introduction (page 4) and in Settings > Voice, and can change it at any time.
- No mercy is the default level: its lines contain swearing and playful insults aimed only at not walking enough. Declared in the age rating (frequent profanity or crude humor, 13+). The lines never refer to body, weight, appearance, health, age, gender or other personal traits. Choosing Normal turns the voice off completely.
- With Spicy or No mercy, a heart in Home says a line about today's steps; tapping it gives another line. Lines also appear in the Joker pop-ups, the goal celebration and the notifications: a few local notifications a day, only between 10 am and 8 pm, none once the goal is reached (Settings > Voice > NEAT notifications: Few, Normal, Lots).
- Lines are chosen on the device (file in section 3).
- Christmas theme, only Dec 1-Jan 6 (not visible during review): snowy Home, holiday icon via setAlternateIconName (standard iOS alert), one gift Joker from Dec 25. Switch: Settings > Theme > Seasonal themes.

6. IN-APP PURCHASES
None. NEAT is free, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads.
