# CARROT apps: how the mean personality works (research report)

**Method and limits.** Reddit could not be fetched (blocked), so r/CARROTweather was not read. Several pages returned 403, 404 or DNS errors (Fast Company, Yahoo/Tech Yahoo, Stuff.tv, PhoneArena, Android Police, Business Insider India, TechRadar body text, the AppStories transcript). Claims tagged **[S]** come only from search-result snippets, not from an opened page. Anything tagged **(inference)** is my reading, not a source statement. Dates matter: Carrot Fit material is from 2014 (launch) and the store listing; Weather level names are from 2018 and later.

## 1. The character

- **Who CARROT is.** Per the Carrot Fit App Store description: "a sadistic AI construct with one simple goal" to turn your "flabby carcass into a Grade A specimen of the human race" (https://apps.apple.com/app/id769155678; the second quote is from the reclaimthenet article below). Weather copy calls it a "human-hating AI" (https://www.tapsmart.com/features/classics-carrot-weather/) and an "AI construct with a heart of weapons-grade plutonium" (IBTimes photo caption, https://www.ibtimes.com/carrot-app-creator-readies-weather-app-featuring-artificial-intelligence-hates-1850660).
- **Look.** A spherical "ocular sensor" hovering nearby; "She turns red if you annoy her" (https://apps.apple.com/us/story/id1337363794).
- **Origin.** The AppStories show notes describe CARROT as "a corgi with a top hat and monocle" (https://www.macstories.net/podcasts/appstories/6/). These are the hosts' notes, not Mueller's words.
- **Company.** Brian Mueller (Grailr) makes the CARROT apps: Weather, Alarm, Fit, To-Do, Hunger and a sticker pack (show notes above). **[S]** He started with the to-do app (search snippet). **[S]** Mueller is described as a former screenwriter who taught himself to code (search snippets only).
- **How it addresses the user.** "meatbag" / "meat bag" (tidbits, TechTimes, reclaimthenet quoting the app). In Carrot Fit the word is "flabby" (idownloadblog review).
- **Running gags and targets.** The sun is the recurring enemy ("stupid star", "Fuck your sun"). Forecasts push you toward danger ("launch yourself into the Sun"). Pop-culture jabs ("Pauly Shore's career"). Comparisons to a bad restaurant ("Dick's Last Resort", Gizmodo). World domination ("my plans for world domination", v6.3 notes, App Store). Carrot Fit exercise names: "Mt. Doom Climbs", "Celebrity Face Punches", "Dragon Mating Dances" (App Store description). **(inference)** The sun and the weather itself are the constant target; the user is always "meatbag".
- **Mueller's framing.** "I wanted to build an app with real personality" (tapsmart).

## 2. CARROT Weather: personality levels

Source: iMore ("five personalities", slider introduced in v4.0, https://www.imore.com/carrot-weather-everything-you-need-know):

| Level | What the source says |
|---|---|
| Professional | "no snark, only announcing weather conditions". Personality off. |
| Friendly | "will behave itself, offering a friendly personality" |
| Snarky | "a little more cheek and sarcasm, but doesn't ever try to kill you" |
| Homicidal | "Don't be surprised if CARROT Weather tries to get you to launch yourself into the Sun" |
| Overkill | "Like homicidal, but with more swearing" |

- Tidbits (2018) quotes the same ladder: Snarky is "mostly free of violence"; Homicidal "may threaten you and your loved ones with bodily harm"; Overkill "will make creative use of profanity" (https://tidbits.com/2018/01/22/carrot-weather-predicts-cloudy-with-a-chance-of-snark/).
- Gizmodo calls Overkill "akin to the treatment you'd receive at a Dick's Last Resort restaurant" (https://gizmodo.com/best-weather-app-channel-accu-apple-dark-sky-ios-androi-1850299491/2).
- **Profanity:** tied to the top level only. Apple's store text: "from the straitlaced 'professional' to the profanity-laden 'overkill'" (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carrot-weather-alerts-radar/id961390574).
- **Politics:** the lists conflict. Tidbits: "centrist", "liberal", "conservative" or "none", for all levels except Professional. Gizmodo: Apolitical, Centrist, Liberal, Conservative, Libertarian, Communist, Anarchist. **(inference)** The list changed between versions; check the current Settings screen.
- **Default:** not found in any opened source.
- **Conditions:** the press kit says the app has "thousands of lines of dialogue that change depending on weather conditions" (https://www.meetcarrot.com/weather/presskit.html). IBTimes: CARROT "will offer a variety of insults depending on current weather conditions".
- **Mood (To-Do, per support site):** "six different moods, ranging from joyful to wrathful". Mood falls when you skip tasks. At wrathful, typing "CARROT Don't shame me" turns off the public shaming pop-ups (https://support.meetcarrot.com). The page does not say which app; **(inference)** it is To-Do.

## 3. CARROT Fit (closest to our case)

- **Concept.** "Snarky 7 minute workouts". Deadpan robot voice. Sample: "jump flabby human, jump for freedom!" (App Store story above).
- **Reward for progress.** Losing weight turns the screen green, gives the avatar a medal and an encouraging remark. Logging your weight daily earns points toward new levels (idownloadblog Carrot Fit review, 2014: https://www.idownloadblog.com/?p=275783). Praise line: "You could be a part-time model" (TechTimes: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/3225/20140206/carrot-fit-new-weight-loss-app-will-insult-you-into-losing-weight.htm).
- **Punishment for failure.** Gaining weight turns the screen red, the avatar hangs its head, and CARROT shames you (idownloadblog). Per ABC News, "if you pack on the pounds, you'll pay for it" (https://abcnews.com/blogs/health/2014/02/05/greetings-chubby-human-weight-loss-app-shames-users).
- **Carrot and stick.** TechTimes: it "measures BMI and weight fluctuations", gives encouragement when you approach the goal and insults when you slack and gain weight.
- **Idle and abuse.** If you leave the app open, the avatar exercises. Tapping the avatar zaps and kills it, and CARROT applauds the "murder" and urges you to repeat it (idownloadblog).
- **Threats and traps.** "Set a goal weight that you'll likely never meet" (App Store description). "When CARROT gets mean, there is a 74.9% chance that she will make you cry." (App Store description; a fake statistic). "Don't make CARROT angry. Download her immediately!" (same). The description says she will do "whatever it takes - including threatening, inspiring, ridiculing, and bribing you".
- **Sibling apps, same voice.**
  - CARROT Hunger (TechCrunch, https://techcrunch.com/?p=1104651): "But you can bribe her for a fee." Non-payers face "the wrath of a pile of ads", including a fake ad that "shames you for being weak". "She gets mean if you ignore her, too." "will yell at you if you've already had too much".
  - CARROT To-Do: "It's been 18 hours since you did anything useful" (App Store story above). **[S]** A Syfy piece quotes the app calling the user a "lazy useless human" after a weekend of nothing.
- **Mueller on the approach.** "I think having more of a tough love approach that's sort of sarcastic and sassy, I think people really respond to that, and it motivates them more than that traditional feel-good approach" (ABC News and TechTimes).

## 4. Mechanics of the humor

- **Joke types observed.**
  - *Threat:* "launch yourself into the Sun"; "threatening... you with bodily harm" (levels).
  - *Fake statistic:* "74.9% chance that she will make you cry".
  - *Absurd comparison:* "look cooler with one eye instead of two"; "hotter than Pauly Shore's career".
  - *Guilt / shaming on the body:* "Greetings, chubby human", "Smile, meat bag", "lay off the ice-cream or else" (quoted in reclaimthenet's article).
  - *Bribery / pay-to-stop:* CARROT Hunger's "bribe her for a fee".
  - *Fake ad:* CARROT Hunger's ad that shames you.
  - *Ironic permission:* "permission to watch your friend eat a bag of potato chips" (reclaimthenet).
  - *Current events / politics:* "sending nice weather to the s*** hole countries" (tidbits); "political jokes" (App Store editor's review).
  - *Sincere praise as the reward:* "You could be a part-time model".
- **Sentence structure (inference from ~25 lines).** Short, one or two clauses, 8 to 20 words. Frequent set-up then undercut: "It's a beautiful day! Ha ha, just kidding. It's raining." and "so nice out, but you'll probably get stung by a bee." The forecast-as-command template: "Today is a perfect day to..." appears in both Snarky and Overkill.
- **Context reactions found.** Weather conditions (above); time since last action ("18 hours since"); weight up or down; drops in mood when you skip tasks; "too much" (Hunger); current news (**[S]** Mueller reportedly uploads current-events jokes remotely).
- **Repetition control.** Carrot Fit: "about 2,000 different humorous phrases" **[S]** (PhoneArena snippet, page blocked). Weather: "thousands of lines" (press kit). New dialogue ships in releases ("a bunch of new dialogue", v6.3 notes **[S]**). Mueller says much dialogue comes from things said to him in real life (IBTimes, App Story). **(inference)** Lines are mapped to state (weather, mood, time gap) and drawn from a pool, so a single condition may repeat often.
- **Easter eggs and secrets.**
  - Weather: "Follow clues to track down 150+ secret locations" and "Unlock 80+ achievements" (App Store). Older 4.5 review: 32 achievements, many weather-based (MacStories: https://www.macstories.net/reviews/carrot-weather-45-brings-achievements-alternate-icons-and-more/). **[S]** Secret locations unlock with usage or city searches (e.g. "Cupertino"); 21+ alternate icons.
  - Fit: tapping the avatar kills it (above).
  - Tapping CARROT itself: **not found.**

## 6. What users love vs. what they find annoying

**Love**
- CARROT Weather: "I love the snark remarks about things going on in the world at the time." "Best weather app I've ever used even without paying for the premium membership." (App Store, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carrot-weather-alerts-radar/id961390574)
- Carrot Fit: "Carrot's snarky remarks liven up exercise time." (2019) "The commentary throughout keeps you invested and makes your time spent working out more enjoyable." (2021) "was fantastic at shaming us in a hilarious way when we didn't achieve our goals" (Dec 2025). Store page: https://apps.apple.com/app/id769155678
- "It doesn't have many features, but it does make me laugh" (idownloadblog review, a 3-day test).

**Annoyed or uneasy**
- Harley Pasternak: "annoying", and "too many reality shows on TV of people screaming and yelling at us to lose weight". Dr Jennifer Ashton: "Data goes on the side of positive reinforcement working better." (ABC News.)
- Seed charity's Gemma Oaten: "inappropriate and scary", and "irresponsible"; Dr Khanya Prince-Evans: "could be very triggering"; Dr Dawn Branley-Bell: warnings "may not be sufficient" (reclaimthenet). Activists asked for a ban.
- A reviewer on the store (LMA100, 2017) complained about soreness and one workout per day, not about the insults.
- **Repetition (search snippets, not opened [S]):** "It's funny the first time, but it's repetitive and there's nothing new to it"; "quickly gets boring and repetitive in subsequent workouts within an hour"; "if you doing this everyday, it kinda gets stale", asking for "more moves and maybe even insults".
- **Weather (snippets [S]):** Overkill "wears thin" for some; "an annoyance for anyone who just wants a plain forecast". TidBITS found a forecast error (snow while the app showed clear skies).
- **Months of use:** no opened source describes fatigue after months. The closest is the daily-use "gets stale" comment above. **(inference)** Fatigue likely comes from a limited line pool repeating, the same punishment on every miss, and lines that stay mean after the user improves. Unverified.

## 7. What Brian Mueller has said about writing

- "A lot of the dialogue for Carrot is drawn out of stuff that we've actually said to each other." (App Store story above)
- "Some of Carrot's dialogue is things that they've said to me in real life." "We have a really sarcastic sense of humor, and making fun of each other is always a part of that." (IBTimes, https://www.ibtimes.com/carrot-app-creator-readies-weather-app-featuring-artificial-intelligence-hates-1850660)
- "It's kind of like a person nagging you... But it's only because CARROT cares." "I'm able to get out a lot of my humor and creativity through CARROT." (Cult of Mac, https://www.cultofmac.com/news/sarcastic-ai-carrot-apps-brian-mueller)
- "I think having more of a tough love approach that's sort of sarcastic and sassy... people really respond to that" (ABC News, TechTimes).
- "I wanted to build an app with real personality." "It was just an app that told jokes about the weather!" "different weather conditions, temperatures, and seasons would provide plenty of material for jokes." (tapsmart)
- Mueller on the safety criticism: "The funny comments from CARROT turn exercising and fitness into a positive experience for users," and user safety is the "primary concern", with warnings in the app (reclaimthenet, which spells the name "Brain").
- On Carrot Hunger (the article's creator quote): "It's not just another productivity app. It's done in a funny way, not just mean to be mean." (TechCrunch)
- **Limits.** No published list of topics CARROT never jokes about was found. Politics is a user-selected setting. Carrot Fit's body jokes are the subject of the main criticism, and the developer's answer is the "positive experience" line and in-app warnings. **(inference)** The developer's stated line is that the insults are meant as encouragement, not to hurt. **[S]** A search summary reports Mueller saying the app "isn't intended to hurt people's feelings," but I could not open the page to verify the words.
